Bible First — Data Deletion Request

Developer: DJMD Studio

Updated: November 16, 2026

This page explains how to delete your information in Bible First (Android package com.djmdstudio.biblefirst) and how to request deletion of information you voluntarily sent to DJMD Studio.

No Bible First account is required or created. Bible study data is normally stored on your own device, not in a DJMD Studio online account.

Request deletion of support messages

If you emailed DJMD Studio a bug report, suggestion, or feedback, you can request that we delete the correspondence and associated personal information we hold.

  1. Email dombibleapp@gmail.com with the subject Bible First — Data Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us which previous messages or information you want deleted. Include enough details to locate the correspondence, such as the sending email address and approximate date. You do not need to send passwords, government IDs, or payment credentials.
  3. DJMD Studio will review your request, may ask for reasonable verification where needed, and process eligible deletions within a reasonable time, subject to applicable legal obligations. We will reply when practicable.
Email a deletion request

What we can delete

Information in support or feedback correspondence received by DJMD Studio, such as your messages, sender or optional reply email address, and the app/device diagnostics included in the messages (app version, platform, operating system version, and device model), where the correspondence can be identified and deletion is permissible.

Delete information on your device

Bible First keeps your bookmarks, highlights, notes, journal entries, devotionals, reading progress, preferences, and app-managed backups locally on your device. You can remove individual entries using available in-app controls or clear Bible First's app storage in Android settings to remove its local data. Uninstalling Bible First normally removes app-managed data.

DJMD Studio cannot remotely access or erase your locally stored study information. Before clearing storage or uninstalling, make sure you have exported anything you wish to keep.

Exported backups and third-party payments

Any JSON backup you saved or shared outside Bible First remains under the control of the destination you chose. Delete those copies from your device, cloud storage, email account, or other destination separately. Such backups may contain your personal writing and are not promised to be encrypted.

PayPal and GCash process optional contributions outside Bible First. Requests concerning records held by those payment providers should be made to the relevant provider under its own privacy and record-retention rules.

What may be retained, and for how long

DJMD Studio may need to retain limited correspondence or records for legitimate legal, security, abuse-prevention, or recordkeeping obligations. Such records are retained only as necessary for the applicable purpose or as required by law, then deleted when that reason no longer applies. There is no promise that every message is automatically deleted within 90 days. If a particular deletion request cannot be fully fulfilled, we will explain the applicable limitation where possible.

Questions

Contact dombibleapp@gmail.com. See the Bible First Privacy Policy for more about data handling.
