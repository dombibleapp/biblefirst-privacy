Bible First — Privacy Policy

Developer: DJMD Studio

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bible First (Android application ID: com.djmdstudio.biblefirst) handles information when you use its Bible reading and personal study features. Bible First is designed for offline, personal use without requiring an account.

1. Information stored on your device

Bible First stores information needed for the features you use, including bookmarks, highlights, personal notes, journal entries, devotionals you write, reading position and progress, preferences, and reminder settings. This information is kept in storage managed by the app on your device. Bible First does not operate a user account, online synchronization service, advertising system, or analytics service for this version.

2. Backups and exports

When automatic backup is enabled, Bible First checks whether a backup is due when the app opens or resumes; it does not create scheduled backups while closed. The app keeps the three most recent automatic backups in its own storage. You can also create a backup manually, export a JSON backup using a share sheet, or save it to a folder you select.

Backup files may contain your personal writing and preferences. They are JSON files and should not be treated as encrypted files. Keep exported copies private. Once you export or share a backup, its storage and further handling depend on the destination you select (for example, a device folder, email service, or cloud-storage provider). Bible First does not control or automatically delete copies you export.

In this Android version, the app disables Android system cloud backup and device-to-device data extraction for its app-managed storage. User-directed exports remain available.

3. Reports and feedback you choose to send

When you select “Report a problem or feedback,” Bible First prepares a message in your email app. The message contains the report type, subject and description you provide, any optional reply email you enter, and basic diagnostics: app version, platform, operating-system version, and device model. Your personal Bible notes, journal entries, and devotionals are not automatically attached.

Nothing is emailed automatically. You review the message and decide whether to press Send in your email app. If you send it, your email provider processes the message and DJMD Studio receives your correspondence at dombibleapp@gmail.com. The sender address, message contents, and information you voluntarily include may become available to us. Correspondence may be retained in our email account to respond and address the issue, subject to applicable legal obligations and deletion requests.

4. Optional PayPal and GCash contributions

Bible First is free to use. Optional contributions do not unlock content, features, or services. If you select PayPal, the app opens an external PayPal page. The GCash option displays a QR image you can scan using a compatible payment app. You may also save that QR image to your device's photo gallery.

Bible First itself does not ask for card numbers, payment-account passwords, or transaction details and does not process payments inside the app. Payment platforms process transactions under their own privacy notices. The recipient may receive transaction details as permitted by the applicable payment service.

5. Permissions and local notifications

If you enable devotional reminders, Bible First uses device notification features to schedule notifications locally. You can change notification settings or permissions through the app and your device settings. The app does not register your device for a Bible First-operated push notification service.

When you choose to save the GCash QR image, Bible First uses the device's photo-saving functionality. On supported devices it may ask for permission to add a picture to your gallery. Bible First does not need broad access to read your personal photo collection for this feature.

6. Network activity and third parties

Ordinary Bible reading and personal writing do not require an internet connection. Bible First does not automatically transmit your reading history, notes, bookmarks, or devotionals to a DJMD Studio server. External services you choose to open—such as PayPal, GCash, an email app, or a backup-sharing destination—handle information under their own policies and may have separate network connections.

7. Storage, security, and retention

Your study data remains in app-managed storage until it is deleted through an available app action, replaced through a restore operation, or removed with the app's local storage. Android uninstall normally removes the app-managed local database and automatic backups. Files you saved or shared outside the app can remain after uninstalling and must be managed separately.

Device-level access controls and the operating system help protect local app storage, but no device or storage method is completely risk-free. Use your device's lock and store exported backups securely. We do not claim that exported JSON backups are encrypted.

8. Requests and privacy questions

There is no Bible First online account to delete. You can manage or remove your local content using the app and your device, and remove exported backup copies at their saved destinations. To ask about feedback messages you sent, request deletion of correspondence we hold, or raise any other privacy concern, email dombibleapp@gmail.com. We will review requests subject to applicable law and any necessary record-retention obligations.

9. Changes to this policy

DJMD Studio may revise this policy if Bible First's features or data practices change. The updated effective date will appear above. Material changes to how your data is handled may require additional notice or consent under applicable rules.
